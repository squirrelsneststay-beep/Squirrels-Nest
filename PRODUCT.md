# Lane End Farm

## Register
**brand** — this is a marketing landing site, not a product UI. Design IS the product. Editorial, slow-paced, image-led.

## Who it's for
Couples (mid-20s to mid-50s) and small families in the UK looking for a quiet weekend or short break in the English countryside. Not a luxury resort audience — these are people who want to feel out-of-step with everyday life, with budget for something thoughtful (£150–250 / night). They book on Airbnb, browse on a phone over breakfast, decide quickly when they see the right place.

## What we're selling
A converted cabin on a working farm at the end of a long lane in the English countryside. Hand-finished, wood-fired, set against open fields. Two bedrooms, sleeps 4, pet-friendly. Operated by Zoe.

## Tone
Quiet. Editorial. Hand-made. Trustworthy. The voice of someone who built the place themselves, slowly. Short sentences, full stops. Never breathless or marketing-y.

Good: "A converted cabin on a working farm. Wood-fired. Hand-finished."
Bad: "Discover your perfect countryside getaway with our luxurious cabin retreat experience!"

## Brand
- **Palette**: warm, earthy, restrained. Cream `#fcfaf6` background, dark ink `#1a1a1a`, soft ink `#555`, muted `#9a9a92`, line `#e5e1d7`. One accent only — moss `#4f6b54` used sparingly for one or two italic words in display headlines.
- **Typography**: EB Garamond / Cormorant Garamond for display. Geist for body and small labels. No mono for UI labels — clean sans only.
- **Photography**: real cabin photos when available. Until then, flat solid tone placeholders (no gradients, no grain, no fake-photo effects). Aspect ratios: 4/5 portrait, 16/10 landscape, 16/9 wide.
- **Motion**: scroll-driven. Slow, generous. Reveals lag slightly behind the wheel. Reduced-motion respected throughout.

## Anti-references
Don't copy savor.it pixel-for-pixel. Don't use purple/blue AI gradients. Don't use emojis. Don't centre-stack everything. Don't add chat widgets, cookie modals, or "Subscribe for 10% off" popups. Don't use any photo we don't own.

## Strategic principles
1. **The photography is 60% of the brand.** Until real photos exist, the site is in placeholder-art mode. Don't add visual noise to compensate.
2. **One signature motion moment, not five.** A pinned scroll story is enough. Resist adding parallax to every section.
3. **The Airbnb deep-link is the only conversion.** No native booking, no contact form upsell. CTA wording is "Check availability" → opens Airbnb listing in a new tab.
4. **Mobile-first.** Most guests will discover this site on a phone. Test at 375px before 1440px.

## Out of scope
- CMS (Zoe won't be editing copy herself)
- Authentication
- E-commerce / on-site booking
- Multi-language
